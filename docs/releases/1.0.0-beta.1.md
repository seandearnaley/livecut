# Livecut 1.0.0-beta.1 — the pad

The first beta of Livecut 1.0. New instances open on an XY pad, Kaoss-style,
in place of the settings pages. Press it to cut, let go to stop on the next
beat or bar, and drag to move between settings of the chosen program. Eight
programs sit beneath it.

This is a pre-release. The legacy pages are still there under **Classic** while
1.0 decides which of them stay (see [What's not final](#whats-not-final)).

## The pad

- **Press** to engage. Cutting starts on the next unit of the program's grid.
- **Let go** and the cuts carry on to the next beat or bar, set by **Beat / Bar**,
  then fade to the input over 5 ms. Program switches land on the same grid.
- **Hold** latches the pad. The latch survives loop wraps, seeks and stop/play.
- **Straight** keeps every cut on even divisions of the beat, so a program plays
  no triplets or other tuplets.
- **Mix** blends the input with the program.
- Each program has a fixed seed and a fixed neutral base, so the same spot in
  the same bar always sounds the same.

## The eight programs

| Program | X | Y | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cut** | Density: 8ths to 32nds, with more repeats | Departure: from near the original order to a full re-edit | CutProc11. The bottom-left is nearly the untouched break, the top-right busy jungle editing. |
| **Bounce** | Triplet feel, with halftime at the far right | Ratchet climb | Triplet bounces against a straight beat, and ratchets that get faster and rise in pitch to close each phrase. |
| **Glitch** | Repeat rate: 1/16 to 1/128 | Pitch step per repeat, falling to rising | SQPusher's fills, through a crusher that gets crunchier as the repeats get faster. |
| **Suction** | Where the band sits, 120 Hz to 5 kHz | How narrow and resonant it is | The cuts squeeze into a singing band that bursts 1½ octaves open on every cut and sucks shut behind it over 180 ms. Level is held, so squeezing never thins the sound. |
| **Filter** | A low-pass from 200 Hz to fully open | How much it rings | The end of every roll strikes a tail on the cutoff's own note. The tail ducks 12 dB under each hit, so the hits stay clean and the tail sings in the gaps. |
| **Tape** | Stop length: ½, 1, 2 or 4 beats | How often: every 4 bars up to every beat | The track plays untouched, brakes to a halt on the grid, then spins back up and lands exactly back on the track. |
| **Loops** | Depth | Loop length: a bar down to a beat | Captures the bar you press in and loops it, folding into shorter loops of its first hit. No shorter loop is louder than the loop it sits in. |
| **Rush** | Drag or rush | Roll length: 1 to 4 beats | The track plays until the last beats of each bar, then a roll repeats that beat's hit into the downbeat: evenly, rushing and rising up to a fourth, or dragging and falling. |

## Clean by design

- Between its stops, rolls and loops, Tape, Rush and Loops play the input
  sample for sample.
- Joins, switches and restarts crossfade: 2 ms inside Rush's rolls and Loops'
  folds, 5 ms when a switch or restart cuts a sound short.
- Switching **Surface** between Classic and Pad while playing doesn't click.
  Classic's cuts release with Classic's own settings, and Classic's first cut
  after an idle pad fades in.

## Everything since 0.2.0

0.3.0 to 0.8.0 were never published. This beta includes all of them, reachable
from the Classic surface: Capture and Save WAV, Hold, Swing and Accent, Studio
lookahead with Attack Align and Reverse, the Impact effects, the Fracture
readers, and Resonance. Their notes are in this folder, from
[0.3.0](0.3.0.md) to [0.8.0](0.8.0.md).

## Compatibility

- The pad parameters are appended with version hint 9. No existing parameter's
  index, ID, range or choices change, so automation from 0.2.0 still lands on
  the same controls.
- The state schema is 9. A session saved before the pad opens on **Classic** and
  sounds the same as before. New instances open on the pad.

## What's not final

- **The legacy pages.** 1.0 will decide which Classic pages stay and whether
  Studio and Spectral remain. That may remove controls before 1.0.0.
- **Listening.** Every program was built to pass the
  [listening audit](../audit/1.0-listening.md)'s bar. Each one's known limits
  are listed in its design spec under `docs/superpowers/specs/`.
- **Windows builds are not code-signed yet.**

## Validation

All 729 tests pass on macOS and Windows CI:
- 235 processor, UI and state cases;
- 494 engine cases.

Pinned output hashes keep Classic and every program bit-identical across
changes, with separate values for Apple Clang and MSVC. Each program had an
independent review, and base-against-head fuzzing of each program found no
change to any other program.
