# Livecut 1.0 listening audit

Started 2026-11-01. This audit decides which of the 0.3–0.9 features belong in a
focused, premium Livecut 1.0. Every feature is judged by ear on real breaks, in
Ableton Live 12, against the bar below. A feature that doesn't clear it is
fixed or cut. "It works and the tests pass" is not enough on its own.

None of 0.3–0.8 has been published, so cutting a feature now breaks no one's
saved projects or automation.

## Setup

- **Build:** `main` at `b60e778` (0.8.0), arm64 Release, installed as
  `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Livecut.vst3`. The installed 0.2.0 build is
  backed up. Perform is the separate *Livecut Perform Prototype* plug-in
  (0.9.0, `184eb26`).
- **Material:** `~/Music/Livecut Audit/` holds `Break Funky 102 bpm.aif`,
  looping on track 3 with Livecut, and `174_StraightBreak_SP_01.wav` for the
  jungle and drum & bass checks.
- **Reset:** delete Livecut from the track and load it again before each audition,
  so every test starts from the defaults.

## The bar

A feature stays only if it meets all of these:

1. **It sounds great on real material**, not just correct. You'd reach for it.
2. **It's clean:** no clicks, zips, aliasing or level jumps you can hear.
3. **It's immediate:** it works while playing. No "stop the transport and
   Apply", no added latency to manage.
4. **It's Livecut:** it strengthens the cutting. An effect any DAW already has
   needs a reason to live inside Livecut.
5. **It earns its controls:** sounding good takes few controls, and the
   defaults already sound good.

Verdicts: **Keep**, **Fix** (worth having, not yet good enough; say what's
wrong) or **Cut**.

## Feedback log

**2026-11-01:** "All of Resonance, Fracture, grain mode and tape mode, mode selection
is not intuitive at all. The UX has to be a lot better, but the XY version had
a bit more of that."

The editor's own wording shows why. These pages expose how the engine works
rather than what you'll hear:
- The Family help reads "GrainCut and TapeCut replace the wet source reader".
- The status line reads "Family: next bar; other settings: next block.
  Frequency Env uses Impact AD".
- Controls depend on other pages. Family overrides the procedure buttons, Tape
  speed switches off Pattern's pitch controls, and the Frequency envelope is
  timed on the Impact page.

This fails bar 5 ("earns its controls"). The XY pads instead name musical
results: Variation, Density, Grain, Tail.

Same session: "The main modes sounded great and there is a lot of good stuff
in there, no clicks."

**What this means:** the sound engine passes and the interface doesn't. 1.0 is
about how the good sounds are reached, not about cutting the sounds themselves.

**2026-11-01, pad slice 1 in Live:** "It sounds pretty good, but I really like the glitch one. Yeah, those three features are good." The owner asked for a way to turn off triplets and loose timing: "sometimes it's better to be on the beat ... they're just kind of too wonky ... sometimes it's better to have like mathematical symmetry."

**2026-11-02, Straight in Live** (`pad/straight` at `38308ca`, Cut, Bounce and Glitch with Straight on and off): "Sounds fine." Verdict: **Keep**.

**2026-11-02, Suction in Live** (`pad/suction` at `1fde8d7`, across the whole pad on the funky break): "Sounds good." Verdict: **Keep**, with the curves as built.
