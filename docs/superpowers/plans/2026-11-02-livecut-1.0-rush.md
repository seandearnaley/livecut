# Livecut 1.0 Rush Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The pad's Rush program. It plays the track, and rolls the hit a fixed number of beats before every bar line into the next downbeat, evenly, rushing or dragging. Every roll lands exactly on the bar line.

**Architecture:**
- A new `Procedure::rush` and `class Rush`, so CutProc11's draws stay untouched. Rush draws nothing.
- `CutParameters` gains `rollBeats`, `rollCurve` and `rollCents`, defaulting to no roll.
- `Cut` gains `fade` (default true). Rush's cuts set it false, so the track joins the input sample for sample.
- The Cutter marks Rush's block plans: they count elapsed time exactly through tempo changes, and a frame after their last cut plays the input.
- `mapPad (rush, x, y)` fills the procedure's fields.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, JUCE 9.0.4, Catch2 3, CMake. Engine code stays JUCE-free.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-livecut-1.0-rush-design.md`. Parent: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-livecut-1.0-pad-design.md`.

> **Updated after the build:** the spec is authoritative.
> - **Task 2's floor is an assert.** The loop that dropped `n` under the floor could never fire: the shortest roll cut is at least 1/24 of a beat by construction.
> - **Task 5 grew a tail.** Its switch tests found that a switch from Rush mid-roll spliced the repeat onto the next program: 4.2× the references to Tape. A restart that cuts off a Rush cut now lets it play on as a `LivePlayer::Tail`, crossfading to what follows over 5 ms, in its own RED/GREEN commit.
> - **Ramps land within 0.1 ms.** Through tempo automation a roll's last cut ends within 2 frames before or 4 after the bar line, not 1.
> - **More pins and tests.** Rush joins both hashes as built. Arrangement loops are tested in the engine and the processor. A separate fuzz checks Rush's partitions, NaN and peak.
>
> **Updated after the second review:** each fix went in as its own RED/GREEN commit.
> - At 44.1 kHz half-sample ties, a Rush block reads from the frame it starts on, and a Rush plan counts a boundary within 10⁻⁹ beats as reached, so the track is the input for every buffer size.
> - A switch out of Rush on one of its block boundaries fades the input out, so it never drops to silence; a switch into Rush fades the last program's cut out.
> - A roll's joins and its hand-back at the bar line crossfade over 2 ms (the owner's ruling).
> - No roll cut is under 30 ms.
> - Classic's accent and transforms don't reach Rush's last blocks.
> - Tests at tie tempos in six meters, beat-quantised switches on held material, four-bar sweeps and ramps every 1 to 2048 frames.

## Global Constraints

- **Classic is unchanged.** `LegacyEquivalenceTests`, the Classic Fracture hashes and every existing test pass unchanged.
- **The other programs are unchanged.** Cut, Bounce, Glitch, Suction, Filter and Tape are bit-identical through the processor and through the Cutter's restart, idle and tempo paths. Pins are taken before any engine change.
  - The exception: tests that used Rush as "the unavailable program" move to Loops.
- **Audio thread:** no allocation, locks or I/O. No new parameters. `Cut` doesn't grow. The `procedure` parameter's choices don't change.
- **Values verbatim from the spec:**
  - phrases of one bar;
  - roll start `B − min (rollBeats, B)`, late rolls from `B − ⌊B − s⌋`;
  - `n = round (4T)`, ratio `8^c`, geometric lengths, the last cut the remainder;
  - floor 1/32 beat;
  - Straight `m = round (3 |c|)`, steps of `½ · 2^−j`, or ¼ for `m = 0`;
  - pitch `rollCents · k / (n − 1)`, swell from −12 dB, even in decibels;
  - mapping `rollCurve = 2x − 1`, `rollCents = 500 (2x − 1)`, `rollBeats = {1, 2, 3, 4}[min (⌊4y⌋, 3)]`, seed 808, 16 units.
- **Style and process:**
  - Allman braces, a space before call parentheses in `src/`, SPDX headers.
  - RED/GREEN for each behaviour.
  - Commit per task, ending the message with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **Bits.** `fade` true must take exactly today's envelope path. Every change in `Cutter` is gated on a Rush plan.
2. **Landing.** A roll ends on its bar line at any tempo, meter, rate and ramp, and no frame drops to silence.
3. **Level.** The swell keeps rolls near the track on real material, drums alone included.
4. **Determinism.** Rush has no state and draws nothing.
