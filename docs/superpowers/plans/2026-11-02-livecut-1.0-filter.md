# Livecut 1.0 Filter Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The pad's Filter program: a level-held resonant low-pass sweep whose rolls end on tuned tails that duck under the hits.

**Architecture:**
- Impact's filter gains an engine-only `sweep` type. It's the `band` path with its high-pass fixed at 30 Hz Butterworth and its own pink-power makeup table.
- `sweep` carries a tail inside the Impact filter: one mono sine, struck by the cut after each roll, tuned to the cutoff's note, ducked at every cut.
- `PadMapping::impact` (from Suction) carries Filter's mapping.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, JUCE 9.0.4, Catch2 3, CMake. Engine code stays JUCE-free.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-livecut-1.0-filter-design.md`. Parent: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-livecut-1.0-pad-design.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Classic is unchanged:**
  - The `lowPass`, `bandPass` and `highPass` outputs are bit-identical to before.
  - All existing tests pass unchanged, including `LegacyEquivalenceTests` and the Classic filter hashes.
  - `impactFilterType` keeps exactly its three choices.
- **Suction is unchanged.** The band's output is bit-identical, pinned by a hash taken before any engine change. Cut, Bounce, Glitch and Suction's tests pass unchanged. The exception is tests that used Filter as "the unavailable program": they move to Tape.
- **Audio thread:** no allocation, locks or I/O. No new parameters. `ImpactEffects` stays trivially copyable, because the route rack copies lanes.
- **Values verbatim from the spec:**
  - high-pass: 30 Hz, q 0.707;
  - makeup: cap 2 (+6 dB), and edges above 18 kHz not counted;
  - tail fold: semitones n in [−9, 15) from A4 = 440 Hz;
  - tail ramps: 2 ms;
  - silence floor: 10⁻⁶;
  - duck: floor 0.25, 2 ms down, 40 ms recovery;
  - mapping: `c = 200 · 90^x`, `q = 0.707 + 2.3 y (1 − 0.5 x)`, `tailLevel = 0.5 y`, `tailDecayMs = 200 + 700 y`, seed 505, stutter .5/.5, repeats 0/0.
- **Style and process:**
  - Match the surrounding style: Allman braces, a space before call parentheses in `src/`, SPDX headers.
  - RED/GREEN for each behaviour.
  - Commit per task, ending the message with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **Suction's bits.** Generalising the band path (two edge qs, a fixed low edge) must not move a single bit of the band. The pinned hash is the check.
2. **The tail at a switch.** It's part of the held filter: no strike and no duck at the cut that fades it.
3. **Determinism.** The tail's note comes from the target cutoff, not the smoothed one, and a silent tail restarts at phase 0.
4. **Bounded level.** The tail's peak comes from the roll's measured RMS, so a pure tone in the material can't make it ring louder than the roll.
5. **Rates.** Every time constant is in ms or Hz. The tail's tuning and decay hold at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz.

### Task 2: The tail

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/engine/ImpactEffects.h`, `src/engine/ImpactEffects.cpp`.
- Test: `tests/engine/ImpactEffectsTests.cpp`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces `Filter::tailLevel` (default 0, [0, 1]) and `Filter::tailDecayMs` (default 600, [50, 2000]).
- Produces `detail::tailNoteHz (cutoffHz)`, and diagnostics `tailHz`, `tailAmplitude` and `tailDuck`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** Helper: a `rollThenSilence` render. It plays pink noise through a two-cut roll (identities 0 and 1 of 2), then strikes with a single-cut block (0 of 1) and feeds silence.
  - `"A tail strikes on the cut after a roll"`: `tailAmplitude` is 0 before; it's non-zero on the strike's first sample after the 2 ms ramp, and not before. There's no strike after single-cut blocks, on a roll's own cuts, with `tailLevel` 0, or while held for a switch.
  - `"A tail is tuned to the cutoff's note"`:
    - `tailNoteHz` for 200, 440, 1000, 5000 and 18000 Hz is G5 784.0, A4 440, B5 987.8, D#5 622.3 and D5 587.3;
    - `tailHz` equals `tailNoteHz (cutoffHz)`;
    - zero crossings over 200 ms of the silent tail give the frequency within 0.5%, at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz.
  - `"A tail decays over its decay time"`: the amplitude falls 60 dB within 2% of `tailDecayMs` (300 and 900) at 44.1 and 96 kHz. Below 10⁻⁶ it's 0.
  - `"A tail's level comes from its roll"`:
    - the peak is `tailLevel · √2 · RMS` of the roll's filtered output, computed in the test from a twin without a tail, within 10⁻⁹;
    - a roll of silence strikes nothing, and silent input stays exactly silent;
    - a full-scale sine on the tail's own note strikes no harder than the roll's RMS allows.
  - `"A tail ducks under every cut"`: at each cut `tailDuck` reaches exactly 0.25 after `rampSamples`, then follows `1 − 0.75 e^(−t / 40 ms)` within 1%.
  - `"A tail never steps"`: through a strike, a re-strike at a new note while ringing, and cuts every 3000 samples, the tail's own step (output minus a no-tail twin) stays ≤ `1.05 · A · (2π f / rate + 2 / rampSamples)`.
  - `"A tail barely lifts the level"`: on pink noise with a two-cut roll ending every bar at 120 bpm, at y 1, the per-bar RMS is within +1.5 dB of a no-tail twin.
  - `"A ringing tail fades whole at a switch"`: a tail rings at full wet, then switches to Cut's Impact, Suction's band and Classic's low-pass. Run `switchType`'s checks: the error against the ramp is under 10⁻⁶, and the step is ≤ 3 ×. The held tail doesn't duck at the fading cut.
  - `"A tail resets cleanly and allocates no memory"`: `reset()`, a zero wet and a switch into a band all clear it. Processing allocates nothing.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them and see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** a trivially copyable private `Tail` in `ImpactEffects`:
  - roll sum, count and ending flag;
  - amplitude, ramp, decay pole, phase and increment;
  - duck, duck ramp and recovery pole.
  - `startCut` calls it only for an unheld `sweep`. `processBand` adds it to the sweep's wet. `resetBand` clears it.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the full suite.** Expected: all pass.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** with the message "Impact: a tuned tail at the end of each roll, ducked under the cuts".
