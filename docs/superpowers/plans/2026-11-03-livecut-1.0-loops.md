# Livecut 1.0 Loops Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The pad's Loops program, the last of the eight. It plays the track for one pass of a loop (a bar, half a bar or a quarter of a bar), then repeats it, folding into shorter loops of its first hit toward the end of each four-pass phrase. Every repeat reads from the loop's first hit, found in the recorded audio, and every join crossfades.

**Architecture:**
- A new `Procedure::loops` and `class Loops`, stateless, with no random draws.
- `CutParameters` gains `loopBars` and `loopDepth`, defaulting to no loop.
- `Cut` gains a one-byte `loopLevel` in its padding.
- `BlockContext` gains `loopCaptured`.
- The Cutter owns the phrase's loop:
  - the capture frame, fixed when the track pass activates;
  - the read position, snapped once to the nearest onset;
  - the level-hold gains, measured once from the recorded loop.
- Replay blocks read from that position as a plain block source, so `LivePlayer::Tail`, run-ons and availability work unchanged.
- Joins, restarts and transport jumps crossfade from the outgoing cut's continuation.
- `mapPad (loops, x, y)` fills the procedure's fields.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, JUCE 9.0.4, Catch2 3, CMake. Engine code stays JUCE-free.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-livecut-1.0-loops-design.md`. Parent: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-livecut-1.0-pad-design.md`.

## Review Focus

1. **Bits.** Nothing outside a Loops plan, or a restart into one, changes. The join and tail gates are the places to look.
2. **The capture.** It is fixed at activation. It never reaches across a transport epoch, and no replay reads unrecorded audio.
3. **Joins.** Every join crossfades, including at block boundaries, run-ons, restarts on a boundary and transport jumps. The track stays bitwise.
4. **Level.** The hold never lifts, holds deep levels to the loop's RMS on drums, and leaves sustained material alone.
5. **Determinism and partitions,** including the 44.1 kHz half-sample ties.

## Test helpers

`tests/engine/LoopsTests.cpp` (new, added to `CMakeLists.txt` next to `RushTests.cpp`) defines:
- `loops (double loopBars, int depth)`: `CutParameters` for Loops.
- `struct Run`: a Cutter at a rate, tempo, meter and partition. It optionally has a fed `OnsetDetector` and a `SourceContext` with a chosen epoch start. It records input, output and, after each buffer, `cutter.loopDiagnostics()`.
- `passStarts (rate, bpm, beatsPerBar, loopBars, bars)`: each pass's first frame, as the Cutter's activation rule computes it (the first frame at or past the grid point minus half a sample).
- `largestStep (samples, from, to)`, `level (left, right)` (as `RushTests.cpp`'s) and `decibels`.
- The materials:
  - `testing::DrumLoop` with no bass, a 55 Hz sine and a 150 Hz sine;
  - the held bass, 55 Hz at 0.6 plus 110 Hz at 0.15;
  - the review's pad chord, 130.8, 261.6, 329.6 and 392 Hz at 0.2 each;
  - a click train: single samples of 0.8 on silence.

`Cutter::loopDiagnostics()` (added in Task 3) returns plain data: `{ std::int64_t captureFrame, readFrame; std::array<float, 4> gains; bool snapped; }`, with −1 frames before a capture.
