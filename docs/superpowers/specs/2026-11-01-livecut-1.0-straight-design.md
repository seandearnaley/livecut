# Livecut 1.0: Straight

Date: 2026-11-01. Requested by the owner after hearing pad slice 1 in Live:
"sometimes it's better to be on the beat than it is to have these kind of
loose beats ... they're just kind of too wonky ... sometimes it's better to
have like mathematical symmetry." Calls made here rather than by the owner are
marked **(our call)**.

## What it does

**Straight** is a switch on the pad, next to Hold. When it's on, every cut
starts and ends on an even division of the beat: quarters, eighths,
sixteenths and finer halvings. There are no triplets, quintuplets, sextuplets
or septuplets. The machine keeps all its other decisions: where rolls go,
repeats, pitch, halftime and phrase lengths.

When it's off, every procedure sounds exactly as it does today, sample for
sample. New instances start with Straight off **(our call)**, so the three
programs the owner approved keep their sound until Straight is chosen.

## Per procedure

| Procedure (program) | Uneven today | With Straight |
|---|---|---|
| CutProc11 (Cut) | Stutter rolls choose 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 cuts per unit | Rolls choose 1, 2, 4 or 8 cuts per unit, with the original weights for those four renormalised (.4, .3, .1, .05 → divided by .85) |
| Bounce (Bounce) | A bounce is 3 × beat/3, or beat·2/3 + beat/3. The closing ratchet is 3 × zone/6, 3 × zone/12, 6 × zone/24 | A bounce is beat/4 + beat/4 + beat/2 (a gallop, where it was 3 × beat/3) or beat·3/4 + beat/4 (dotted, where it was 2/3 + 1/3) **(our call)**. The ratchet is 2 × zone/4, 2 × zone/8, 4 × zone/16, with the same three climbing pitch steps |
| SQPusher (Glitch) | 13 transcribed fills, 7 of them tuplets (thirds, fifths, sixths, sevenths) | Only the 6 fills built from even divisions: table entries 0, 1, 2, 4, 9, 11 |
| WarpCut | Geometric accelerando rolls | Not used by any pad program. Unchanged in this slice |

A cut sitting an odd number of units long (CutProc11's 1, 3 or 5 units) is
still on the grid. It's syncopation, not looseness, and stays **(our call)**.

## Random draws

- With Straight off, every draw is exactly as today.
- With Straight on, the choices change but the number of draws in each
  decision stays the same where the structure allows it:
  - one weighted draw for a roll speed;
  - one integer draw for a fill;
  - Bounce's same `chance` calls.
- The ratchet's `sweep` draws once per cut, so a straight ratchet (8 cuts)
  draws fewer times than today's (12). Straight phrases are deterministic and
  repeatable, but they aren't the same phrases as with Straight off.

## Parameter

- `padStraight` is a bool, "Pad Straight", default off, version hint 9,
  appended at index 113 after `surface`.
- None of the pad parameters have been published, so appending another hint-9
  parameter breaks no one's sessions.
- `Settings.padStraight` reaches the engine through
  `CutParameters::straight`, which `padSettings` sets.
- Classic always uses `straight = false`.

## Interface

- A "Straight" toggle with component ID `padStraight` sits in the bottom row
  between Hold and Beat/Bar. It matches Hold's style.
- Tooltip: "Keeps every cut on even divisions of the beat: no triplets".
- It takes effect from the next block, like the other pad settings, so there
  is no restart.

## Quality gates

- **Straight off:** all existing tests and the legacy equivalence tests pass
  unchanged.
- **Straight on:** for each procedure, over many seeded phrases, every cut's
  length in beats × 64 is a whole number. 1/64 of a beat is the finest even
  division the pad's grids reach: 32 units per bar × 8 cuts per unit.
  Every block also keeps the existing block contract.
- **By ear:** the owner compares the three programs with Straight on and off.
